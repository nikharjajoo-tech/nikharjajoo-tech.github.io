About

The bridge between the model and the business.

I spent nearly six years at Deloitte learning how large enterprises actually run: procurement at scale, 7,000+ contract migrations, and the politics of getting a room of stakeholders to say yes. Then I moved to the frontier as AI Product Manager at Grey Chain AI, and most recently joined Attri AI, where I prototyped a GenAI claims-denial triage agent that turns a 45–60 minute appeal review into a decision in about 60 seconds.

I've shipped RAG platforms, AI report builders, and due diligence engines for banks and global consulting firms, and I build my own AI products on the side. My job is translation: turning ambiguous AI ambition into PRDs, sprints, governance frameworks, and adoption numbers that leadership signs off on.

The work I'm proudest of is the unglamorous kind: proposing CBAC over RBAC to a bank's leadership and getting it formally adopted, or finding the ingestion bottleneck at 100 MB and driving it to 300 MB before it killed production viability.

Relentless work ethic is not a line on my resume. It's answering the load-test question at 11 pm, rewriting the UAT runbook until consultants stop asking questions, and demoing to C-suite until the adoption curve bends.
